component gallery

Every component the design language has to keep working, rendered from the shipping stylesheet with frozen data. This page is what the visual baselines photograph.

Brand

The mark is the console's own glyph for escalated — the ^ it prints the moment the agent decides a human is needed — drawn as a solid wedge so it reads equally as a spark. That is the only forge the identity gets. Shown at the sizes it actually has to survive: a browser tab is 16px.

State, severity, risk, decision

State is never colour alone. Every one of these carries a glyph and a word beside it; colour is the third channel and never the only one. Photographing the whole vocabulary is what makes that rule enforceable rather than aspirational.

Hard component 2 — a finding with its evidence citation

Hard component 3 — the budget meter

Thresholds are drawn on the track, not implied by the fill colour. The fill is clamped and the printed number is not: a window sitting at 140% is a different fact from one sitting at 100%.

tokens · this hour 34.0%

Form controls

The base input, select, textarea and button rules, which nothing else in this gallery reaches. They are here because a type-scale change that only touches form controls would otherwise be invisible to every baseline on this page — which is exactly the gap that let two near-duplicate font sizes coexist unnoticed.

Callouts

!! My alert path is broken. Until the watchdog returns, an empty incident list means nothing.
+ resolved — the workload recovered and verification closed it.
- suppressed — a storm circuit breaker is open.
x quarantined — the action was rolled back and the workload isolated.
~ retrying — this spends money when pressed.
!! error — the investigation could not complete.
